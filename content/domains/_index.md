- **数论** (Number Theory) — EP11, EP29, EP32
- **分析 / 偏微分方程** (Analysis / PDEs) — EP02, EP15, EP28, EP33
- **信号处理** (Signal Processing) — EP30, EP35, EP37, EP38, EP39, EP40, EP41, EP42
- **信息论** (Information Theory) — EP07, EP21, EP22, EP23, EP24, EP27
- **拓扑** (Topology) — EP08, EP14, EP23
- **非线性动力学** (Nonlinear Dynamics) — EP09, EP31, EP34
- **调和分析** (Harmonic Analysis) — EP02, EP36, EP40
- **统计 / 机器学习** (Statistics / ML) — EP22, EP25, EP41, EP42
- **物理声学** (Physics / Acoustics) — EP02, EP09, EP15, EP28, EP29
- **博弈论** (Game Theory) — EP18, EP25
